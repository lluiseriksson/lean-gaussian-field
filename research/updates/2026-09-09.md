## Source cards

### On overlap concentration in the Curie-Weiss Random Field model

- Source: [arXiv:2609.08169v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08169v1); Li, Yutong, Sandhu, Juspreet Singh, Shi, Jonathan.
- Dates: first 2026-09-08; latest 2026-09-08. **new-this-calendar-week**.
- Evidence: **literature / preprint**. Reviewed: Abstract and submission history.
- Reported result: A Curie-Weiss random-field result gives sub-Gaussian overlap tails under beta<1 and a specifically matched Gaussian field law, via Laplace analysis and a moment-generating-function bootstrap.
- Limits: The field law and matching condition are part of the result. Mean-field replica overlap concentration does not yield spatial covariance decay or Yang-Mills small-field estimates.

### Thick points of log-correlated Gaussian fields do not depend on the mollifier

- Source: [arXiv:2609.08132v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08132v1); Ambrose, Kyle.
- Dates: first 2026-09-08; latest 2026-09-08. **new-this-calendar-week**.
- Evidence: **literature / preprint**. Reviewed: Abstract and submission history.
- Reported result: Ambrose reports almost-sure independence of thick-point sets from admissible mollifiers for log-correlated Gaussian fields, including a two-dimensional domain GFF extension.
- Limits: Admissibility conditions and the distribution-valued field matter. This is not a dimension-uniform finite massive covariance-decay theorem or a Wilson-flow smoothing bound.

### Height fluctuation for Lozenge Tilings of Polygons

- Source: [arXiv:2609.04789v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04789v1); Huang, Jiaoyang.
- Dates: first 2026-09-04; latest 2026-09-04. **new-in-trailing-week**.
- Evidence: **literature / preprint**. Reviewed: Abstract and submission history.
- Reported result: Huang proves Gaussian-free-field fluctuations for lozenge tilings of a specified class of simply connected polygons, using uniform inverse-Kasteleyn asymptotics.
- Limits: Connected liquid region and non-forcing boundary assumptions are required. A massless GFF scaling limit cannot supply exponential decay for a massive finite Gaussian model.

### Uniform Partition-Function Estimates for Coulomb Modulated Energy at All Positive Temperatures

- Source: [arXiv:2609.00867v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00867v1); Wang, Zhenfu, Zhao, Xianliang.
- Dates: first 2026-09-01; latest 2026-09-01. **background**.
- Evidence: **literature / preprint**. Reviewed: Abstract and submission history.
- Reported result: Wang and Zhao report particle-number-uniform partition-function bounds for centered Coulomb-type modulated energies in a locally square-integrable regime, with a second-chaos Fredholm-determinant limit.
- Limits: Positive-real-parameter integrability does not establish complex zero-freeness. Particle-number uniformity is not automatically RG-scale, support or volume uniformity in the Yang-Mills activity norm. First posted 1 September.
